# The Knight in the Panther's Skin (ვეფხისტყაოსანი) — Scrollytelling Realm Network & Full Bilingual Edition

An interactive **Scrollytelling Swimlane Realm Network Graph** and **Complete Bilingual Digital Edition** for Shota Rustaveli's *The Knight in the Panther's Skin* (*ვეფხისტყაოსანი*), designed for [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) and offline local browsing.

## Key Features
- **Swimlane Realm Network Architecture (D3.js v7)**:
  - Anchors 21 characters across 7 geographical realms (`არაბეთი / Arabia`, `გამოქვაბული / Wilderness & Cave`, `ინდოეთი / India`, `ხატაეთი / Khataeti`, `მულღაზანზარი / Mulghazanzar`, `გულანშარო / Gulansharo`, `ქაჯეთი / Kadjeti`).
  - Smoothly transitions character nodes and curved Bezier interaction edges as the reader scrolls through **26 Narrative Keyframe Episodes** or **All 65 Chapters**.
- **8-Type Emoji Interaction Taxonomy**:
  - Filter bonds in real time by `💍 მიჯნურობა (Courtly Love)`, `🤝 დაძმობილება (Sworn Brotherhood)`, `👑 მეფობა / ყმობა (Fealty & Kingship)`, `🛡️ მსახურება (Loyal Service)`, `💌 წერილი (Epistle / Letter)`, `⚔️ ბრძოლა (Combat & Conflict)`, `🔓 დახსნა (Alliance & Rescue)`, and `💧 თანაგრძნობა (Shared Mourning & Empathy)`.
- **Complete Unabridged Bilingual Corpus (`data/poem-bundle.js` & `data/chapters/`)**:
  - **1,669 Georgian stanzas (6,676 lines)** from the 1966 Akaki Shanidze & Aleksandre Baramidze critical edition ([Anbani.db / Georgian Wikisource](https://github.com/Anbani/anbani.db/blob/master/datasets/vefxistyaosani.csv)).
  - **1,576 English stanzas** from Marjory Scott Wardrop's 1912 translation ([Allgeo Digital Archive](https://www.allgeo.org/index.php/en/266-shota-rustaveli-the-knight-in-the-panther-s-skin)).
  - **Scholarly Commentary & Monograph**: Grounded in works by Academician Elguja Khintibidze ([Georgian Encyclopedia](https://www.georgianencyclopedia.ge/en/form_eng/903)), Prof. Zaza Khintibidze ([Tbilisi State University](https://e-learning.tsu.ge/mod/resource/view.php?id=111669)), Academician Aleksandre Baramidze ([TSU](https://e-learning.tsu.ge/mod/resource/view.php?id=98600)), and the [National Parliamentary Library of Georgia](http://www.nplg.gov.ge/civil/vefkhistkaosani.html).
- **Interactive Inspector, Global Search & Language Modes (`KA+EN`, `KA`, `EN`)**:
  - Click any character node or emoji edge to inspect character status, active bonds, exact stanza quotes (`stanza_ref`), and one-click links into the full bilingual stanza reader.
  - Search instantly by stanza number (`1–1669`), chapter title, or Georgian/English text.
